# Trust Model

ATLAS treats trust as a system property created through controls, evidence, and
transparent limitations.

## Trust dimensions

### Security

Protect systems, identities, secrets, and data from unauthorized access or
abuse.

### Privacy

Limit data collection, use, retention, exposure, and sharing to justified
purposes.

### Compliance

Map system behavior and evidence to applicable organizational, contractual, or
regulatory requirements.

### Reliability

Ensure systems behave predictably and recover safely under failure.

### Testability

Make important behavior independently verifiable.

### Transparency

Communicate limitations, uncertainty, automation boundaries, and residual risk.

## Trust invariant

Claims about safety, compliance, performance, or correctness require evidence.
Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
