# Engineering Economics Model

Engineering decisions consume capital, time, attention, operational capacity,
and future flexibility.

## Economic dimensions

### Delivery cost

Implementation effort, review effort, migration effort, and coordination cost.

### Operating cost

Infrastructure, tooling, support, observability, incident response, and
maintenance.

### Delay cost

Value lost while a capability remains unavailable or a risk remains unresolved.

### Change cost

Difficulty of extending, migrating, testing, or replacing the solution.

### Risk cost

Expected impact of failure, security events, data loss, non-compliance, or
operational instability.

### Option value

Future flexibility preserved by modularity, reversibility, portability, and
clear contracts.

## Decision principle

The cheapest implementation is not always the lowest-cost decision. ATLAS
evaluates total lifecycle cost and strategic flexibility.
