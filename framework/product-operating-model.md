# Product Operating Model

ATLAS treats product development as a sequence of explicit decisions rather
than a stream of implementation requests.

## Product lifecycle

```text
Problem discovery
    ↓
Outcome definition
    ↓
Opportunity framing
    ↓
Solution exploration
    ↓
Scope and sequencing
    ↓
Delivery
    ↓
Measurement
    ↓
Learning
```

## Product artifacts

- Problem statement
- User and stakeholder context
- Desired outcome
- Constraints
- Success metrics
- Acceptance criteria
- Risks and assumptions
- Rollout and measurement plan

## Product decision principles

- Start with the problem, not the feature.
- Separate outcomes from outputs.
- Make assumptions visible.
- Define who benefits and how.
- Preserve strategic focus.
- Instrument meaningful outcomes.
- Prefer reversible experiments when uncertainty is high.
