# Architecture Maturity Model

The ATLAS Architecture Maturity Model helps teams assess whether architecture
is accidental, documented, governed, measurable, and continuously improved.

## Level 0: Unmanaged

- Architecture is implicit.
- Ownership is unclear.
- Decisions are undocumented.
- Changes rely on individual memory.
- Operational risk is discovered in production.

## Level 1: Documented

- Major systems and repositories are mapped.
- Critical integrations are documented.
- Basic ownership exists.
- Important decisions begin to use ADRs.

## Level 2: Standardized

- Shared architecture principles exist.
- Contracts and quality gates are defined.
- Delivery follows repeatable workflows.
- Approved patterns and exceptions are visible.

## Level 3: Governed

- Architecture changes receive proportionate review.
- Portfolio dependencies are understood.
- Technical risk and debt are tracked.
- Decisions have owners and review triggers.

## Level 4: Measured

- Architecture outcomes are linked to delivery, reliability, cost, and product metrics.
- Platform adoption and duplication are measured.
- Migration progress and control effectiveness are observable.

## Level 5: Adaptive

- Architecture evolves through evidence.
- Governance is automated where practical.
- Standards adapt without losing traceability.
- Teams can move quickly within safe boundaries.

## Usage

Maturity is assessed per capability or domain, not as a single vanity score.
The purpose is to identify the next valuable improvement.
