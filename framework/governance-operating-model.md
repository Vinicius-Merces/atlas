# Governance Operating Model

Governance enables consistent decisions without turning every change into a
committee process.

## Governance layers

### Principles

Stable decision guidance applied across projects.

### Standards

Expected technical and operational practices.

### Controls

Specific mechanisms that prevent, detect, or correct risk.

### Reviews

Human or automated evaluation of evidence.

### Exceptions

Time-bounded, owned deviations with explicit risk acceptance.

### Metrics

Signals that show whether governance improves outcomes.

## Proportionality

Governance effort should match the impact and reversibility of the decision.

## Good governance characteristics

- Clear ownership
- Explicit decision rights
- Fast low-risk paths
- Strong high-risk evidence
- Documented exceptions
- Expiration and review triggers
- Measurable control effectiveness
