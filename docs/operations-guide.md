# Operations Guide

## Before release

- Confirm deployment process.
- Confirm environment configuration.
- Verify monitoring and alert ownership.
- Validate migrations and rollback.
- Prepare runbooks for high-risk systems.

## During incidents

- Prioritize stabilization.
- Preserve evidence.
- Communicate clearly.
- Avoid speculative destructive changes.
- Track decisions and timestamps.

## After incidents

- Validate full recovery.
- Produce a blameless report.
- Track corrective actions.
- Update memory, runbooks, tests, and architecture records.
