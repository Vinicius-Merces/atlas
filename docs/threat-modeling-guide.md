# Threat Modeling Guide

## Begin with assets and boundaries

Threat lists are more useful when grounded in what must be protected and where
trust changes.

## Include abuse

Security failures may involve valid features used in harmful ways, not only
technical vulnerabilities.

## Keep models current

Review threat models when data flows, authentication, permissions, integrations,
deployment boundaries, or model/tool capabilities change.

## Track residual risk

Mitigations reduce risk. They do not erase uncertainty. Remaining risk needs an
owner and review trigger.
