# Knowledge Management Guide

## Keep one canonical source

Duplicate facts drift. Prefer one canonical note with links from related
documents.

## Store provenance

Knowledge should identify where it came from and who owns its accuracy.

## Link decisions to systems

ADRs, risks, integrations, and services should form a navigable graph.

## Review by change

In addition to scheduled reviews, update knowledge whenever architecture,
ownership, contracts, or product policy changes.

## Obsidian usage

The `obsidian/` directory contains a lightweight vault structure. Markdown
links and frontmatter allow project knowledge to remain useful in Obsidian,
GitHub, and AI retrieval pipelines.
