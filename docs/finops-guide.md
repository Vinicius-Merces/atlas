# FinOps Guide

## Make cost attributable

Resources without ownership are difficult to optimize and easy to ignore.

## Connect cost to value

Unit economics are more useful than raw spend when they connect technology cost
to customers, transactions, workloads, or product outcomes.

## Protect reliability

Cost optimization should preserve service objectives and critical user
journeys.

## Measure realized savings

Estimated savings become credible only when implementation and post-change
measurement confirm them.
