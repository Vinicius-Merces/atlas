# Governance Guide

## Optimize for safe speed

Good governance creates fast, supported paths for common low-risk work and
stronger evidence for high-risk work.

## Make exceptions visible

Exceptions should be owned, justified, time-bounded, and reviewed.

## Test controls

A documented control may not be effective. Validate whether it actually
prevents, detects, or corrects the intended risk.

## Retire obsolete governance

Standards and controls that no longer address a meaningful risk should be
updated or removed.
