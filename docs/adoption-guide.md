# Adoption Guide

## Start small

Most projects do not need every ATLAS capability on day one.

## Prioritize pain

Adopt the components that address current failures, confusion, risk, or repeated effort.

## Keep existing strengths

ATLAS should integrate with useful current practices rather than replacing them automatically.

## Validate representative tasks

Before expanding adoption, use ATLAS on real feature, bug, release, and review work.

## Measure outcomes

Track whether adoption improves clarity, quality, speed, onboarding, and operational confidence.
