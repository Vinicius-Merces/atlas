# Generative Engine Optimization Delivery

## Trigger
A public business needs a material GEO/AI-search visibility initiative, not merely a metadata edit.

## Sequence
1. Load project memory, entity sources, current SEO/analytics evidence, and target market.
2. Assign `generative-engine-optimization-strategist`; map entities with `entity-authority-mapping`.
3. Audit rendered discoverability, technical SEO, structured data, and answer gaps.
4. Create evidence-backed content/information-architecture changes; preserve accessibility, locality, and localization.
5. Define baseline and observation protocol with `ai-search-measurement`.
6. Validate browser rendering, technical SEO, schema truth, and independent content discoverability review.
7. Record sources, dates, observations, limitations, and no-guarantee statement.

## Completion
Delivered changes have factual ownership, human-readable/crawlable evidence, a measurement baseline, and no unsupported claim that AI selection or citation is guaranteed.
