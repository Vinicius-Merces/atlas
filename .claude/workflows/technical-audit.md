# Technical Audit Workflow

## Trigger

Independent assessment is needed for risk, acquisition, modernization, release,
compliance, or strategic planning.

## Sequence

1. Define audit objective and scope.
2. Establish evidence requirements.
3. Inspect architecture, code, delivery, operations, trust, and knowledge.
4. Record findings and severity.
5. Validate factual findings.
6. Identify missing evidence.
7. Produce recommendations.
8. Review conclusions with stakeholders.
9. Track remediation separately.
