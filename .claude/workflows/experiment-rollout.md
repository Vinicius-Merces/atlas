# Experiment Rollout Workflow

## Trigger

A product change should be evaluated through controlled exposure.

## Sequence

1. Define hypothesis and decision.
2. Define treatment, control, and population.
3. Validate instrumentation.
4. Review privacy and ethical constraints.
5. Launch limited exposure.
6. Monitor exposure integrity and guardrails.
7. Analyze according to the predeclared plan.
8. Decide to adopt, iterate, or stop.
9. Document learning.

## Rules

- Do not redefine the primary metric after viewing results.
- Do not ignore harmful guardrail movement.
- Do not claim causality from uncontrolled observations.
