# Privacy Review Workflow

## Trigger

A change collects, uses, stores, derives, exposes, or shares personal data.

## Sequence

1. Inventory affected data.
2. Confirm purpose and necessity.
3. Review access and sharing.
4. Review retention and deletion.
5. Review user notice and control.
6. Run privacy impact assessment.
7. Define mitigations.
8. Record residual risk and approval.
