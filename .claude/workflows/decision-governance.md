# Decision Governance Workflow

## Trigger

A decision affects architecture, policy, risk, cost, shared platforms, or
multiple teams.

## Sequence

1. Define decision scope and owner.
2. Identify decision rights and required reviewers.
3. Gather constraints and evidence.
4. Compare credible alternatives.
5. Record selected decision and consequences.
6. Define implementation and controls.
7. Define exception and review triggers.
8. Publish decision to canonical knowledge.
9. Track follow-up actions.

## Completion criteria

- Decision owner identified
- Evidence recorded
- Alternatives considered
- Consequences explicit
- Review triggers defined
