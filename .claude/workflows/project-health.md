# Project Health Workflow

## Trigger

A team needs a current view of technical and operational health.

## Sequence

1. Define scope and stakeholders.
2. Run project health assessment.
3. Validate evidence with owners.
4. Record unknowns.
5. Prioritize critical and high-impact findings.
6. Create improvement actions.
7. Assign owners and review dates.
8. Publish scorecard.
9. Schedule reassessment.
