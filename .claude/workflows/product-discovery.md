# Product Discovery Workflow

## Trigger

A request is broad, strategically important, or contains significant product
uncertainty.

## Sequence

1. Frame the problem.
2. Identify users and stakeholders.
3. Review business and product memory.
4. Define desired outcomes.
5. Identify assumptions and unknowns.
6. Explore solution options.
7. Define scope and non-goals.
8. Define metrics and acceptance criteria.
9. Hand off to architecture and delivery planning.

## Completion criteria

- Problem is explicit
- Users are identified
- Outcomes are measurable
- Scope is bounded
- Major assumptions are visible
