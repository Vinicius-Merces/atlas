# Incident Response Workflow

## Trigger

Production behavior causes meaningful user, data, security, or availability
impact.

## Sequence

1. Declare incident and severity.
2. Assign incident lead and communication owner.
3. Capture impact, timeline, and evidence.
4. Stabilize the system.
5. Mitigate or roll back.
6. Validate recovery.
7. Monitor for recurrence.
8. Produce incident report.
9. Track corrective actions.

## Priorities

1. Protect people and data.
2. Reduce user impact.
3. Restore safe service.
4. Preserve evidence.
5. Understand root cause.
6. Prevent recurrence.

## Rules

- Do not conceal uncertainty.
- Do not destroy useful evidence.
- Do not prioritize blame over recovery.
