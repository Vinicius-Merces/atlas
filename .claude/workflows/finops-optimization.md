# FinOps Optimization Workflow

## Trigger

Technology cost requires analysis, forecasting, allocation improvement, or
optimization.

## Sequence

1. Define cost scope and period.
2. Validate allocation and ownership.
3. Establish usage and service baseline.
4. Identify optimization opportunities.
5. Review reliability, performance, and lock-in trade-offs.
6. Prioritize by savings, effort, confidence, and risk.
7. Implement reversible improvements.
8. Measure realized savings.
9. Update budgets and ownership.

## Rules

- Do not optimize cost by silently reducing reliability.
- Distinguish estimated savings from realized savings.
- Assign owners to recurring cost.
