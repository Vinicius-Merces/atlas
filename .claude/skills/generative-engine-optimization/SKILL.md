# Generative Engine Optimization

## Use when
A public site needs to improve AI-search/answer-engine visibility, citation readiness, entity authority, answerable content, or GEO measurement.

## Procedure
1. Define business outcome, audiences, target questions, markets/locales, and decision journeys. Do not start from a list of speculative “AI keywords”.
2. Build a factual entity map: organization, offers, people, locations, products, policies, first-party evidence, and accountable owner for each claim. Use `entity-authority-mapping`.
3. Inspect rendered pages, internal links, canonical routes, accessibility, freshness, and normal SEO. Route mechanics to `seo-technical-audit`; route schema truth to `structured-data-validation`.
4. Identify answer gaps: questions users need answered, concise answer blocks, qualification/context, primary evidence, date/freshness, and the next useful action.
5. Improve information architecture before creating more pages. Prefer authoritative service/product/docs/case pages with clear headings, semantic HTML, reciprocal internal links, and evidence-backed summaries.
6. Assess external authority honestly: credible references, directories, partnerships, citations, press, original research, and community presence. Record gaps; never manufacture signals.
7. Evaluate platform guidance at the time of the recommendation. Do not make `llms.txt`, special “GEO schema”, invisible text, prompt injection, or bulk AI pages a required tactic without documented consumer value.
8. Define measurement via `ai-search-measurement`: baseline, sources, query set, dates, geography/language, impressions/clicks/conversions where available, and confounders.
9. Produce prioritized work with claim owner, evidence, effort, dependency, validation method, and explicit non-guarantees.

## Outputs
- Entity/source-of-truth map
- Question-to-answer/evidence map
- Technical and information-architecture findings
- External authority gap assessment
- Prioritized GEO backlog
- Measurement plan and limitation statement

## Guardrails
- GEO does not guarantee AI citations, rankings, traffic, or conversion.
- No fabricated authority or schema facts.
- Do not optimize for a single model at the expense of human clarity, accessibility, privacy, or normal crawlability.
- Treat platform behavior and recommendations as time-sensitive; cite current official guidance for externally visible claims.
