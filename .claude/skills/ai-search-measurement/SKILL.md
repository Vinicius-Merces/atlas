# AI Search Measurement

## Procedure
1. Define conversion and discovery outcomes before choosing metrics.
2. Capture a dated baseline for organic traffic, qualified conversions, indexed URLs, branded/non-branded queries, and relevant engagement.
3. Build a representative query set by audience, intent, language, geography, and decision stage; document how each query was observed.
4. Record answer-engine observations separately from analytics: engine/version, signed-in state, locale, timestamp, prompt, citations/links, and volatility.
5. Use Search Console or platform-provided AI reporting when available; do not assume availability or causal attribution.
6. Compare cohorts over a meaningful period, annotate releases, campaigns, seasonality, index changes, and instrumentation changes.
7. Report observed changes, confidence, and alternatives—not “GEO caused X” without defensible evidence.

## Output
Baseline, query log, observation protocol, dashboard/event requirements, reporting cadence, and limitations.

## Guardrails
Do not collect or publish personal data, bypass platform controls, scrape prohibited surfaces, or represent a one-time model answer as durable market position.
