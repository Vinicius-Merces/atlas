# Security Review Gate

## Review questions

- What assets are exposed?
- Where are the trust boundaries?
- Are authentication and authorization correct?
- Are inputs validated?
- Are outputs safely encoded?
- Are secrets protected?
- Are logs free from sensitive data?
- Are dependencies acceptable?
- Are abuse and denial scenarios considered?
- Is residual risk documented?

## Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Changes required
- Blocked
