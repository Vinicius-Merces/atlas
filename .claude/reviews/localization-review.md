# Localization Review Gate

## Review questions

- Are strings externalized?
- Is translation context sufficient?
- Are plural and grammatical variants supported?
- Are locale formats correct?
- Does text expansion work?
- Are right-to-left layouts supported where needed?
- Is fallback behavior safe?
- Was locale QA performed?

## Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Changes required
- Blocked
