# UX Review Gate

## Review questions

- Is the user goal clear?
- Is the information hierarchy understandable?
- Are actions predictable?
- Are loading, success, empty, and error states covered?
- Is the experience keyboard accessible?
- Is focus behavior correct?
- Is content concise and useful?
- Does responsive behavior preserve task completion?
- Is motion purposeful and optional where necessary?

## Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Changes required
- Blocked
