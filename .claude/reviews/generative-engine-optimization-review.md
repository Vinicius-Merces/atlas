# Generative Engine Optimization Review Gate

## Scope
Independently review GEO work for factual authority, usefulness, crawlability, claim provenance, measurement integrity, and platform-specific overclaiming.

## Required evidence
- Entity/source-of-truth ledger
- Rendered page and internal-link evidence
- Technical SEO and schema evidence where changed
- Current official guidance used for AI-platform claims
- Baseline/query-observation protocol and dates
- Content claim owners and freshness

## Review questions
- Does each material answer or entity claim have a visible, accountable factual source?
- Are the changes useful to the intended human audience without requiring AI-only tactics?
- Are canonical routes, locale variants, rendered content, internal links, metadata, and schema consistent?
- Are external-authority gaps recorded rather than simulated?
- Does measurement distinguish observed behavior from causality and model volatility?
- Are `llms.txt`, special markup, generated pages, or model testing presented only with documented value—not as ranking guarantees?

## Outcome
Record `Approved`, `Approved with conditions`, `Changes required`, or `Blocked`. Unsupported public claims, fabricated authority, or missing material evidence require changes before approval.
