# Integration Review Gate

## Review questions

- Are provider, consumer, and owners known?
- Is the contract explicit?
- Are authentication and permissions correct?
- Are timeouts, retries, and idempotency defined?
- Are rate limits understood?
- Are errors observable?
- Are sandbox and production differences documented?
- Is versioning and deprecation defined?

## Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Changes required
- Blocked
