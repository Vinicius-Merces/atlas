# Enterprise Architecture Review Gate

## Review questions

- Is the business capability clear?
- Does an existing system or platform already provide it?
- Is ownership explicit?
- Does the change increase fragmentation?
- Are cross-domain data and integration impacts understood?
- Is target-state alignment clear?
- Is transition sequencing realistic?
- Are retirement and migration implications addressed?

## Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Changes required
- Blocked
