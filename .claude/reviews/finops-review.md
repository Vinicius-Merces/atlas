# FinOps Review Gate

## Review questions

- Is the cost baseline reliable?
- Is ownership and allocation clear?
- Are savings estimates supported?
- Are performance and reliability preserved?
- Are commitment and lock-in risks understood?
- Is implementation effort included?
- Is realized savings validation defined?
- Is recurring cost ownership assigned?

## Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Changes required
- Blocked
