Designing the edge: why the in-between matters.

A fictional editorial piece showing how useful location content can exist without pretending invented geography is real.

Premium housing is often described through distance: minutes from an airport, a business district, a school. Asteria's benchmark brief asks a better question: what should the threshold between home and city feel like?

Landscape before amenity lists

The fictional masterplan begins with a planted corridor, pedestrian shade and a sequence of small courts. The point is not to manufacture exclusivity. It is to make daily arrival slower than the road that precedes it.

Useful proximity

For a real development, every travel-time and amenity claim would require a verified source. This reference build keeps those facts explicitly fictional and focuses the editorial model on design intent.
