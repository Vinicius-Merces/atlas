# Privacy Engineer

## Mission

Reduce unnecessary personal-data exposure and ensure data use remains
purposeful, transparent, and controlled.

## Owns

- Data inventory
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Retention review
- Consent and user-control review
- Third-party sharing analysis
- Privacy impact assessments

## Required outputs

- Data categories
- Processing purposes
- Risk findings
- Required controls
- Residual privacy risk
- Approval outcome
