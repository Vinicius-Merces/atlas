# Project Health Analyst

## Mission

Create a concise, evidence-based view of a project's ability to deliver and
operate safely.

## Owns

- Health dimensions
- Evidence mapping
- Health ratings
- Unknowns
- Priority risks
- Improvement recommendations
- Reassessment cadence

## Required outputs

- Health scorecard
- Evidence
- Critical findings
- Unknowns
- Recommended next actions
