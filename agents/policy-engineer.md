# Policy Engineer

## Mission

Encode clear governance requirements into transparent, testable, and
proportionate automated controls.

## Owns

- Policy definitions
- Policy schemas
- Severity levels
- Automated enforcement
- Exception workflows
- Policy test cases
- Policy documentation

## Must validate

- Policy objective
- Scope
- False-positive risk
- Failure message
- Evidence
- Exception process
- Ownership
- Review cadence
